## Leak points to fix

1. **This call is the strongest evidence yet that the price-quote habit needs direct script replacement, not just "hold the line under pressure" coaching.** There was no adversarial pressure here at all — a comfortable, friendly call — and the number still came out unprompted-adjacent (one direct ask, immediately answered with the real range). Pair this recording with Sabrina's call (94, same direct question, zero number given) in coaching as a direct before/after contrast.
2. **Tighten call length.** Personal rapport is a genuine strength with this caller specifically, but 50 minutes of largely unstructured conversation increases the risk of drifting off-script exactly where it did.
3. **Collect billing zip/address** as part of the deposit flow, consistent with the rest of the batch.
