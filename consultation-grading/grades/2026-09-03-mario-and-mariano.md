## What to keep

- **Objection-handling content quality.** The substantive answers to a genuinely tough, well-informed line of skeptical questioning (evidence base, insurance rationale, safety, diagnosis philosophy) were accurate, calm, and non-defensive — this is worth studying for *content*, even though the call structure around it failed.
- Efficient, well-organized discovery across two children in parallel without losing track of either kid's specific details.

## Leak points to fix

1. **This is a missing-ask call, not a weak-ask call — the most severe version of dimension 3's failure mode in the batch.** Every extended Q&A section needs a bridge back to booking ("Great question — and that's exactly what we'll walk you through at the new patient exam. I've got Tuesday at 3 or Thursday at 10 — which works better?"). Right now the call has no structural skeleton pulling it back toward a close.
2. **The price-ballpark cave happened on the very first direct push**, with no attempt at the "I'd be guessing, and I respect your decision too much to do that" hold-the-line line from the scorecard.
3. **Never let a call end on "we'll reach back out."** At minimum, pin a specific callback day/time or offer to text a link to book directly — an open-ended "call us whenever" is a booking that statistically never happens.
