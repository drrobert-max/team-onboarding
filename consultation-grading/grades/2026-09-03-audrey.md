## What to keep

- Specific, well-organized discovery around a complex medication history (stimulant → non-stimulant switch) without getting lost in the clinical detail.
- Patient, flexible scheduling negotiation that still landed a concrete date and time despite several blocked days.

## Leak points to fix

1. **Always use the $300→$67 anchor.** Saying "it's just $67" without ever naming $300 first throws away a piece of built-in urgency the script is designed to use — an easy, no-cost fix.
2. **Don't let a mid-errand moment become a missed deposit.** A parent standing in a checkout line is often the *easiest* moment to grab a card number, not the hardest — or at minimum, pin an exact callback time ("I'll call you at 5 when you're home") rather than "make it in the office."
