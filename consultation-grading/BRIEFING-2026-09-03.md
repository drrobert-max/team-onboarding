## The three findings

**1. The price-quote leak survived the script coaching, but its trigger narrowed.** After batch 1's fix (replace unprompted recitation with the 4-beat RoF deflection), unprompted recitation is essentially gone. But a specific, narrower version of the same failure reappeared: **when a parent frames the cost question as budget planning** — "so I know if I can afford it," "give me a ballpark before we decide" — rather than skeptical pushback, Becky gives the real number. This drove 4 of the 5 leaks this batch (Brandon, Mario & Mariano, Mika'el, Samaia & Van). Meanwhile she held the line cleanly against much harder, more adversarial cost questions on Canyon, Sabrina, Jayce, Jonathan, and Mateo. **The fix isn't "hold the line harder" — it's specifically drilling the polite, cooperative version of the ask, since that's the one that gets through.**

**2. Two calls this batch are as good as coaching material gets.** Canyon (95) held the line through three separate direct cost questions on a complex, emotionally loaded case. Morrow Family (95) closed and deposited all four family members in one stacked booking. Both belong next to Aries from batch 1 as the "this is what right looks like" reference calls.

**3. One deposit miss looks like a rep failure rather than a customer constraint.** Most missed deposits this batch were genuine — no card in hand, waiting on a spouse, no funds until payday (Audrey, Brandon, Jeremy, Jonathan, Leandro). Kayson is the exception: great-grandma offered to pay on the call and Becky talked her out of it, deferring to the day of the appointment instead. That's a real, coachable leak — a willing payer got turned away from paying.
