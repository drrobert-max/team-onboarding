## Combined trend across both batches (n = 43, mean 66)

- **The core price-quote failure has changed shape.** Batch 1's failures were largely
  Becky reciting the full pricing block *unprompted*, straight from the old v1 script.
  In batch 2, after script coaching, unprompted recitation is gone — but a new,
  narrower trigger replaced it: **a parent framing the cost question as budget
  planning** ("so I know if I can afford it," "give me a ballpark before I decide"),
  rather than adversarial pushback. This exact pattern drove 4 of the 5 price leaks in
  batch 2 (Brandon, Mario & Mariano, Mika'el, Samaia & Van) — Becky holds the line
  against skeptical or hostile cost questions (Canyon, Jayce, Sabrina, Jonathan, Mateo
  all show this working correctly) but caves specifically when the ask sounds
  reasonable and cooperative. The 5th leak (Grayson) was the one AI-chatbot-sourced
  lead in the batch — worth watching whether that channel's leads ask differently.
  **Coaching fix:** drill the 4-beat framework as the answer to *every* ballpark
  request, explicitly including the polite, budget-planning phrasing — not just
  hostile "just give me a number" pushback.
- **The two best calls in the whole 43-call dataset are both from this batch:**
  Canyon (95) and Morrow Family (95). Canyon held the line through three separate
  direct cost questions on a complex, emotionally loaded case; Morrow Family closed
  and deposited all four family members in one stacked booking. Both are strong
  candidates for the training script alongside Aries from batch 1.
- **A second intake rep, Stephanie, appears in the raw call data (Corey, 81 — tracked
  separately above).** Worth a full batch of her calls before drawing any conclusion
  about her skill relative to Becky's; this one call was a low-difficulty, already-booked
  sibling add-on.
- **Deposit misses in this batch skew toward genuine "no card in hand right now"
  moments (Audrey, Brandon, Jeremy, Jonathan, Leandro) rather than a rep failure** — but
  Kayson is a clear exception: great-grandma offered to pay on the call and Becky
  talked her out of it, deferring to the day of the appointment. That's a real leak,
  not a customer constraint, and worth flagging on its own.
- **Compliance flag (out of rubric, needs owner attention):** on the Kayson call, the
  caller volunteered a vaccine-timing comment which Becky did **not** affirm or engage
  with — a clean contrast against the batch 1 Kion finding where Becky did affirm a
  similar claim. Good sign the earlier coaching point landed; worth confirming this is
  consistent, not a one-off.
- **Three non-standard call types required contextual scoring, not standard-call
  penalties:** second-child add-ons where the ask/deposit were already secured
  elsewhere (Eliana), an adult patient reached through a parent where correctly
  declining to book without the patient's own consent is the right call, not a failure
  (Mateo), and genuine hardship/scheduling disqualifications where no real booking
  opportunity existed (John, Lucas). None of these should be read as booking failures
  in the raw score.

  On Ryker, Kion, Alonzo/Gianno, and Savannah/Caleb, Becky delivered the identical pricing block
  ($300→$67 → "2–3 visits/week for ~12 weeks" → "$3,000–$6,000" → "$1,000 off

  three times completely unprompted. On Savannah/Caleb she even named the product
  ("twelve-week behavior reset"), and the quote *demonstrably caused* the objection:
  the mom asked to hear the number again, said "it is steep," and deferred to her
  husband — live, on the call. She is still running the **v1 consult script** where
  quoting the plan was standard. Fix the script she's reciting and most of the
  failures in this batch likely disappear. This is a training/rollout gap, not a
  willpower gap — and it means one coaching session with the new script could move
  her fastest of any possible intervention.

  territory opened via the insurance question, and she stayed clean: individualized
  plan, exact price in the RoF video, discount mentioned without a number, booked and
  deposited. Play Aries next to Ryker in coaching; the contrast is the lesson.

  first, then gave a ballpark on the second ask. Drill the scorecard's hold-the-line

  no-risk holder.

  commitment) is the template — coach *that* version against the Jamie version.
- **When she does reach the close, mechanics stay excellent:** 4-for-4 collecting the
  $67 on calls that booked; times confirmed back; confirmations set. Consistent
